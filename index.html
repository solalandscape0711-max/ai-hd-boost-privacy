Privacy Policy

AI HD Boost is an Android photo-enhancement application developed and operated by solascape. This policy explains how AI HD Boost accesses and handles information when you use the app.

Photos and image processing

AI HD Boost accesses only photos that you explicitly select through the Android system photo picker. Selected images are used to provide photo enhancement, 2× upscaling, monochrome rendering, cropping, adjustments, comparison, export, and sharing features.

Image enhancement is performed on your device. solascape does not operate a server that receives or stores your selected photos, and does not have access to your original or enhanced images.

Local storage and retention

AI HD Boost may store project information, adjustment settings, thumbnails, and processed images locally on your device so that you can reopen recent projects. This app-specific information is retained until you delete project history or uninstall the app, subject to Android system and backup behavior.

Images that you intentionally save to your device gallery remain there until you delete them from the gallery. Clearing AI HD Boost project history does not delete separately exported gallery images.

Export and sharing

AI HD Boost transfers an image to another app or service only when you explicitly choose an export or sharing action. The receiving app or service handles that image under its own privacy practices.

Third-party services

AI HD Boost uses Google Play services to provide certain on-device media enhancement functionality. Google Play services may handle technical or service-related information under Google's applicable terms and Privacy Policy.

Advertising, analytics, and data sales

The current version of AI HD Boost does not display third-party advertising and does not integrate app analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. solascape does not sell users' photos or personal information.

Children

AI HD Boost is not directed to children. Its Google Play target audience is limited to adults aged 18 and over.

Security and data minimization

AI HD Boost is designed to minimize data collection by processing photos on the device and by using Android system interfaces for photo selection, export, and sharing. The app does not use this website to collect data, and this privacy-policy page contains no cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if AI HD Boost introduces new features, services, analytics, advertising, or other changes to its data-handling practices. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.
