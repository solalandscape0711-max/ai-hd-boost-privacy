1. The Service

Pixel Form is a photo utility application for iPhone that allows you to upscale and reframe images you already own into social-ready assets and print-ready files.

Typical use includes:

  • Selecting a Social Pack (approximately 2× upscale and multiple social formats) or Print Pack (approximately 4× upscale and print outputs);
  • Importing images from the Photos library or Files;
  • On-device image enhancement and composition;
  • Adding user-supplied overlays (logos, QR codes, watermarks, stamps, signatures) and editable text;
  • Exporting social formats to Photos or print PDF/PNG to Files or Photos;
  • Storing project records in a local Library.

The App processes images primarily on your device. It does not generate commercial brand assets (such as logos, QR codes, official stamps, or signatures) for you—you must supply those.
