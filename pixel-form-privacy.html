This Privacy Policy describes how Zhang Fan (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in connection with the Pixel Form iPhone application (the “App”). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Policy.

1. Overview

Pixel Form is an on-device photo utility. Most image processing occurs locally on your device. We do not operate a user account system and do not claim to train cloud AI models on your photos.

2. Information We Access or Process

Depending on how you use the App, we may access or process the following:

Photos Library

  • Purpose: Import images you select and, when you choose, save exported social or print images back to Photos.
  • Access occurs only with your permission and only for the images you select or export.

Files / Document Picker

  • Purpose: Import images or save PDF and PNG files when you choose Files as the destination.

On-Device Image Processing

  • Purpose: Upscale, enhance, crop, reframe, and compose layers (including your logos, text, and other overlays) entirely or primarily on your device.

Local Project Library

  • Purpose: Store project metadata and associated image files on your device so you can return to previous work. This data remains on your device unless you delete it or remove the App.

Purchase and Entitlement Status

  • Purpose: Determine Free versus Pro access via Apple StoreKit. We receive confirmation of purchase or subscription status from Apple; we do not receive your full payment card details.

Advertising

The current version of the App does not display third-party advertising. It does not request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission, does not access your device’s advertising identifier, and does not use your activity to track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information described above solely to:

  • Provide and operate the App’s features;
  • Process your image imports, enhancements, and exports at your direction;
  • Manage Free and Pro entitlements;
  • Respond to support requests you send us;
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Project data and imported or exported images are stored locally on your device. We do not maintain a cloud copy of your photo library or projects as part of the core service described in this Policy.

You may delete projects within the App or remove the App to delete local data. Support communications you send to solalandscape0711@gmail.com may be retained as needed to respond to you and for legitimate business records.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Apple StoreKit: Handles in-app purchases and subscriptions.
  • We do not control third-party websites or services you may reach via links.

6. Your Choices and Rights

  • You control Photo Library and Files access through iOS Settings.
  • You may restore purchases, manage subscriptions, or delete the App at any time.
  • Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain personal data, or to object to processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at solalandscape0711@gmail.com.

Because most photo processing and project storage occur on device, many data requests are best handled directly by you on your device.
